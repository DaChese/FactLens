FactLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

FactLens is a Chrome extension that captures audio from your browser tab, transcribes it in real time, and analyzes it for factual claims and language bias. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and what is never stored.

What data FactLens processes

  • Audio from the active tab. When you start a session, FactLens captures the audio stream from the tab you are currently viewing. This audio is split into short clips (approximately 6 seconds each) and sent to our backend server for transcription.
  • Transcript text. The transcribed text is sent to our backend for fact-checking and bias analysis. Short excerpts (~150 words) are sent to third-party AI services (Groq) and a web search service (Tavily) to produce verdicts and analysis results.

What FactLens does NOT do

  • We do not store audio recordings. Audio clips are processed in memory and discarded immediately after transcription.
  • We do not store transcript text. Text is processed in memory and not written to any database or log file.
  • We do not collect any personally identifiable information (name, email, location, etc.).
  • We do not track browsing history or which websites you visit.
  • We do not sell or share any data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not capture audio from your microphone — only from the tab's audio output.

Third-party services

FactLens uses the following third-party services to process your data. By using FactLens, you agree to their respective privacy policies.

  • Groq (privacy policy) — used for audio transcription (Whisper) and language analysis (LLaMA). Audio clips and transcript text are sent to Groq's API for processing.
  • Tavily (privacy policy) — used for web search to verify factual claims. Extracted claim text is sent to Tavily's search API.

Permissions used

  • tabCapture — to capture audio from the active browser tab for transcription.
  • storage — to store session state locally so the panel stays in sync if the service worker restarts. No personal data is stored.
  • sidePanel — to render the FactLens UI in Chrome's native side panel.
  • offscreen — to run audio capture in a hidden document, which is required by Chrome's MV3 architecture.

Data retention

No audio or transcript data is retained after your session ends. The in-memory claim cache on the backend server (used to avoid re-checking the same claim twice) is cleared whenever the server restarts and is never written to disk.

Children's privacy

FactLens is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the new version at this URL.
